It’s The 1975 season.

A fan site for the months when it is dark by half four, the roads are wet, and this band sounds exactly right.

The glass has steamed up. Wipe it.

Nobody official ever called them a cold-weather band.

The internet did that. Search the tags and it is all there: autumn playlists with the slow ones at the top, “tumblr autumn” edits in black and white, a winter song about the drive home two days before Christmas, and people announcing every October that it is The 1975 season again.

The calendar mostly agrees. Four of the five albums came out between September and February.

What gets pinned

We went through the Pinterest boards and the TikTok tags. The same handful of things come up every time: black and white, a box, neon, rain on glass, a coat, a record.
